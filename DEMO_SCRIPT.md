# Demo video script (about 2.5 minutes)

Record the browser at http://localhost:3000 (or :3001) with a screen recorder (on macOS: Cmd+Shift+5, "Record selected portion").
Image generation takes about 45–60 s per image, so either **run the campaign once before recording** and record a second, live run while cutting the waits, or speed up the waiting parts 4–8x in editing.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Empty app | "Generative AI can make a campaign in minutes, but brands can't ship it: the product drifts, the copy makes claims, prompts borrow other people's IP. Campaign Engine fixes that with a brand guardian." |
| 0:15–0:35 | Click **Game launch week**, upload the product photo | "One brief and one product photo. This brief deliberately pushes us toward the biggest game launch of the year: exactly where brands get into IP trouble." |
| 0:35–0:50 | Brand guardian panel: the rules and the protected names | "These are the brand, legal and channel rules, plus names that must never appear. The creative agents never see these. Like an agency, creatives create and a separate reviewer enforces." |
| 0:50–1:10 | Generate strategy, show the insight and 3 concepts, pick one | "The creative director agent writes the insight, the key message and three concepts, and records exactly what the product looks like." |
| 1:10–1:35 | Pre-flight before/after | "Before any image is made, the compliance reviewer checks the plan. Here it caught references to the protected names and rewrote only those lines. The channel limits are counted in code, not guessed." |
| 1:35–2:05 | Production: hero, then 9:16 and 16:9, rule checks, an attempt being repaired (if any) | "fal generates the hero, then the other formats from it. Every image is checked against the original photo and the rules. If the cap or logo drifts, the repair agent rewrites the prompt and regenerates, up to three times, then hands it to a human." |
| 2:05–2:20 | The video spot playing | "The 9:16 frame becomes a video spot with fal image-to-video." |
| 2:20–2:35 | "Brief to campaign in mm:ss" clock, click export, open the JSON | "Brief to campaign in minutes, and an audit trail of every rule result and every attempt, with an AI-generated disclosure." |

Tips:
- Choosing a concept starts plan, pre-flight and production automatically, so start talking about pre-flight as soon as you click.
- If no image needed a repair in your run, show the attempt history of whichever asset has one, or skip that sentence.
- Keep the terminal and `.env.local` off screen.
