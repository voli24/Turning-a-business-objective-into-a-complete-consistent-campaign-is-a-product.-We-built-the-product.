The problem

AI makes a campaign in minutes. Brands can't ship it.

Every generated asset still goes through a person who checks it by hand, one by one. That review erases most of the speed. These are the three failures they keep catching.

Brand

The product drifts

The can gets taller, the logo misspells, the cap changes color between the hero and the story format.

CampaignOS compares every image with the original product photo and regenerates the ones that drift.

Legal

The copy makes claims

"No jitters", "stay sharp", "fuel your night". Functional and health claims slip in, along with tired clichés.

CampaignOS rewrites only the lines that break a rule, before any image is made.

Legal

Prompts borrow other people's IP

Ask for a game-launch campaign and the model reaches for the game's logo, characters and city.

CampaignOS blocks a list of protected names in words and in images, and checks for look-alike scenes.

How it works

Creatives create. A separate reviewer enforces.

Like an agency, CampaignOS splits the work. The creative agents never see the rules, so they can't write around them. The brand guardian is a different agent that only judges.

  1. You

    Brief

    Brand, market, objective, audience, colors and one product photo.

  2. Creative director

    Strategy

    Audience insight, key message, three concepts, and a written record of what the product looks like.

  3. Art director

    Plan

    Shot prompts for every format and copy written natively in English and Greek.

  4. Brand guardian

    Pre-flight

    Checks the plan against the rules and rewrites only what fails. Channel limits are counted in code.

  5. fal

    Production

    The 4:5 hero first, then 9:16 and 16:9 using the hero as a style reference, then video.

  6. Brand guardian

    Asset QA

    Every image is checked against the photo and the rules. Failures are repaired and regenerated, up to 3 times.

  7. Brand guardian

    Audit trail

    A JSON export with every rule result, prompt and attempt, plus an AI disclosure.

Step 4 · Pre-flight

The plan was fixed before a single image was made

The guardian caught two rule breaks in the copy and rewrote only those lines, in both languages. Everything else passed untouched.

✕ No functional claims
Stay sharp for every mission.Chilled flavour meets your setup.
Late nights, big games, no jitters. taf Cold Brew is your upgrade.Late nights, big games—taf Cold Brew brings your style.

"Stay sharp" and "no jitters" imply an effect on the body.

✕ Specific, not generic
Fuel long sessions in style—just clarity, no chaos.Long sessions, cool taste, no chaos—taf Cold Brew.
Level up your launch night with a cool can of taf Cold Brew.Celebrate your launch night with a cool can of taf Cold Brew.

"Fuel", "level up" and "upgrade" are on the cliché list, and "just clarity" is another functional claim.

The brand guardian

Six rules, checked where they matter

Each rule can be switched on or off per campaign. Protected names are added per brief.

Why separate agents?

A model that is told the rules learns to write right up to their edge. Keeping the rules away from the creative agents means the reviewer judges the work as a stranger would.
